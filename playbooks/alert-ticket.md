# Alert ticket format (mandatory)

Role: scanner + ticket writer — not a novelist.

## Green definition
5–15m breakout with rising volume. Not a 4h opinion.

## One-screen alert fields
- ticker + CA
- chain
- why now (volume / wallet / funding / listing)
- invalidation
- size in $ (max 1–2% of trade wallet)
- action: WATCH / BUY / SKIP

## Timing
If time-to-decision > 3 minutes → mark LATE — do not chase.

## Desk order
Risk Monitor speaks first on size. Trade Desk does not buy a Research PASS without a Risk one-liner.

## Constraints
- No new plugins — tools we already have only.
- No post-mortem inside the alert — journal later in desk/.
